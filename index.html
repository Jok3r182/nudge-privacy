Privacy Policy

Nudge · last updated 22 October 2026

The short version: Nudge collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. Everything you write, send, share and plan is stored in your own iCloud account, where only you and the person you paired with can read it.

What Nudge stores, and where

When you pair with someone, Nudge creates a private shared space in your iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. The content of that space — your notes, moods, photos, shared songs, plans, grocery list, birthdays and anniversary — is stored there and synced to the other person's device. A copy is kept on each device so the app and the widget work without a connection.

This data is held under your own Apple Account. I have no access to it, no ability to read it, and no copy of it on any server of mine.

What Nudge does not do

  • No account creation, no email address, no password, no phone number.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no profiling.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Photos

If you attach a photo, Nudge asks for permission to read the one you choose from your photo library. The photo is resized, stored in your iCloud space, and shown to the person you paired with. Nudge does not read the rest of your library and does not upload anything you have not chosen.

Apple Music

If you share a song, Nudge asks for permission to use Apple Music so you can search its catalog. Your searches go directly to Apple and are never sent to me. Nudge does not read your music library, your listening history or your subscription details.

The song you pick (its title, artist, album art and Apple Music link) is stored in your iCloud space like any other nudge, and shown to the person you paired with. Previews and the full song are played by Apple. Apple's handling of Apple Music data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Firebase

Nudge includes Google's Firebase core library, which only connects the app to its Firebase project. Analytics, crash reporting, advertising and messaging features are not used. Nudge does not send your notes, photos, songs, plans or any other content to Firebase, and does not use it to identify or track you. Google's handling of Firebase is described in the Firebase privacy information.

If any Firebase feature that processes data is added in the future, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Nudge sends them when your partner sends you something, and before plans, birthdays and your anniversary. These are delivered by Apple's Push Notification service. Reminders for your own plans and dates are scheduled on your device.

Who can see your content

Only you and the one person you paired with. Apple operates the iCloud storage and sync; their handling of that data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the local copy from that device. To remove the shared space itself, delete Nudge's data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Since I hold no copy, there is nothing for me to delete on your behalf.

Children

Nudge is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
